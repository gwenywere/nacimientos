Source: INE, Movimiento Natural de la Población, anonymised microdata: births (one record per live birth) and deliveries (one record per delivery, with every baby's outcome). Children per woman uses women by single year of age on 1 July from INE table 56934; by mother's origin, women by age and nationality or country of birth from INE table 56939 (from 2002; 1 January for 2025, the latest date it has). "Rest of Europe" is all women from abroad minus Africa, the Americas, Asia and Oceania. Births with no origin recorded are left out of the origin lines.

INE changed the record layout in 1980, 1996 and 2007; dotted lines mark a change where it affects a chart. 1975–79 records legitimacy instead of marital status, and has no weights. The 1975–79 deliveries files cover multiple deliveries only, so stillbirths start in 1980. Before 1996 the files record neither nationality nor country of birth.

Quirks in the files themselves, left as they are: caesareans drop to about 18% in 2008 and 2009 (26–27% in the years around them), which looks like incomplete recording; in 2007 the share of first children jumps by four points and fathers' mean age dips; in 2007 and 2008 the mother's country of birth is filled in only when it is not Spain, so a blank is counted as Spain.
